## 1. Final positioning

| English | العربية |
|---|---|
| Basset Daddi is an independent growth strategist and builder based in Algeria. He designs and runs a business's paid media, web experiences and automation as one measurable system — not as a set of disconnected services. | باسط دادي استراتيجي نموّ مستقلّ يعمل من الجزائر. يصمّم ويدير الإعلانات المدفوعة وتجارب الويب والأتمتة لأيّ شركة كنظام واحد قابل للقياس — لا كخدمات متفرّقة. |

Frame (internal):

- **Category:** growth systems — not "marketing services", not "web development".
- **For:** founders and marketing leads who want growth engineered, not improvised. Algeria first, international second.
- **Against:** agencies that sell deliverables and hours; single-skill freelancers; "AI" tool-sellers.
- **Proof standard:** real projects with real context; results only when verified.

## 5. Service architecture

Three pillars, each scoped by the outcome it must produce. No menu of fifteen items, no pricing on the site in V1. Most engagements start in Growth and pull in the other two.

| Pillar | Outcome | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **GROWTH** · **النموّ** | New customers, profitably. · عملاء جدد، بربحية. | Paid media (Meta, TikTok, Google) · Customer acquisition strategy · Conversion strategy · Testing — الإعلانات المدفوعة (Meta، TikTok، Google) · استراتيجية اكتساب العملاء · استراتيجية التحويل · الاختبار |
| **DIGITAL EXPERIENCES** · **التجارب الرقمية** | From attention to action. · من الانتباه إلى الفعل. | Websites · Landing pages · Conversion experiences — المواقع · صفحات الهبوط · تجارب التحويل |
| **SYSTEMS** · **الأنظمة** | Runs without you. · يعمل من دونك. | AI workflows · Automation · Operational systems — سير عمل بالذكاء الاصطناعي · الأتمتة · الأنظمة التشغيلية |

## 6. Brand personality

Core three: **Precise · Calm · Execution-oriented.** Supporting: strategic, technical, ambitious, modern, curious, human, premium.

Confident, not loud. Technical, not jargon. Ambitious, not grandiose. Premium through restraint, not decoration. Human, not casual.

## 7. English tone of voice

- First person singular. "I", never "we".
- Short sentences, one idea each. Verbs over adjectives.
- Concrete nouns: paid media, landing page, automation, test, measure.
- No superlatives; no adjectives that praise the author.
- Numbers only when real and verified.
- Plain words over industry words; industry words over invented ones. American spelling.
- Banned: the brief's list, plus *leverage, empower, journey, passionate, cutting-edge, end-to-end, "at the intersection of", "in today's fast-paced world"*.

| Not this | This |
|---|---|
| We help brands unlock their full potential through innovative digital solutions. | I build the systems that make growth measurable. |
| Passionate about delivering seamless experiences. | Websites built to convert, then tested until they do. |
| Our team of experts | I work directly with you. |

## 8. Arabic tone of voice

- Authored in Arabic, not translated. A sibling of the English, not a twin.
- Modern Standard Arabic in a business register; no dialect in core copy.
- First person: أبني، أعمل، أقيس. Short sentences, verbs early.
- No bureaucratic constructions: يتم، القيام بـ، الحرص على، «من خلال» as filler.
- Industry terms in Arabic where natural (الإعلانات المدفوعة، صفحة هبوط، أتمتة، التحويل); Latin only where Arabic would feel forced (ROAS, CAC, Meta Ads, n8n).
- Western digits; Arabic punctuation (، ؛ ؟); no letter-spacing, no uppercase device.

| ليس هكذا | بل هكذا |
|---|---|
| يتم تقديم حلول رقمية مبتكرة من خلال فريقنا. | أبني أنظمة نموّ عملية. |
| نحن شغوفون بتحقيق النجاح لعملائنا. | نقيس ما يهمّ، ونحسّنه. |
| شركة رائدة عالمياً في التسويق الرقمي. | من الجزائر، لمشاريع مختارة. |

## 10. What Basset Daddi should NOT communicate

1. **Agency scale** — no "we", "our team", "offices", "clients worldwide".
2. **Unverified numbers** — no ROAS, revenue, client counts or years of experience unless real.
3. **Guru energy** — no motivation, no "transform", "unlock", "next level".
4. **AI as the promise** — AI is a tool inside Systems, never the headline.
5. **Generalism** — no "full-service", no fifteen unrelated services.
6. **Urgency tricks** — no "2 slots left", countdowns or manufactured scarcity; availability is stated plainly.
7. **Guarantees** — no promised results.
8. **Self-praise adjectives** — innovative, passionate, world-class, cutting-edge, seamless.
9. **Fake proof** — no testimonials, logos or case studies without the client's real context.
10. **Location inflation** — Algeria is a fact, not a claim about reach.
